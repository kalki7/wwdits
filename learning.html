The Vocabulary of Solitude

Title Vocabulary Frequencies (Top words: help, suicide, need, can't, kill, die)
Selftext Narrative Frequencies (Top words: can't, friends, help, school, family, job)

Visualizing term frequencies reveals a clear linguistic boundary between Titles and Selftexts. Titles are compressed cries: heavily populated by action verbs and acute crisis terms like "help", "suicide", "kill", and "die". Conversely, the inner selftexts contain highly descriptive, relational words: detailing social units and external constraints ("friends", "school", "family", "job", "work"). This suggests that while the internal crisis (the "body") is deeply embedded in social and vocational friction, the announcement (the "title") is condensed into raw, immediate physical danger.

The Compressed Blueprint

To facilitate rapid development and high-frequency analytical loading without parsing the massive 711 MB raw JSONL database, we constructed a compressed tabular layout: derived_data.csv (179 MB).

This tabular format enriches each post record with character counts, word counts, and a normalized heuristic sentiment score (calculating normalized relative densities of positive and negative valence lexicons):

derived_data.csv (Schema Layout): --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- id: Sequence Index (e.g., POST-000001, POST-000002) title_length_char: Raw length of title in characters title_length_word: Raw word count of title selftext_length_char: Raw length of body in characters selftext_length_word: Raw word count of body has_selftext: Boolean flag (1 = non-empty body text, 0 = empty) sentiment_score: Heuristic sentiment score [-1.0000 to +1.0000] title_clean: Normalized lowercased title without punctuation selftext_clean_short: Cleaned lowercased preview (first 200 characters) of the body text ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
